# Ridmika Ekanayaka | Portfolio

A personal portfolio website showcasing my background, skills, projects, and certifications as a Software Engineering student focused on Quality Assurance, UI/UX Design, and Business Analysis.

**Live site:** https://ridmika23.github.io/My_Portfolio/

## About

I'm a final-year Software Engineering student at Sri Lanka Technology Campus (SLTC), with a strong interest in business analytics and UI/UX design, along with working knowledge of Quality Assurance. This site is my digital resume — a place to share who I am, what I've built, and how to get in touch.

## Features

- Responsive single-page layout with smooth section navigation (Home, About, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Contact)
- Dedicated resume page (`resume.html`)
- Downloadable CV (`cv.pdf`)
- Project showcase with tech stack tags
- Certifications timeline
- Contact section with direct links to Email, LinkedIn, and GitHub

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5** — page structure
- **CSS3** — styling (`style.css`)
- **JavaScript** — interactivity (`script.js`)
- **Font Awesome** — icons
- **Google Fonts** (Sora, Space Grotesk) — typography

## Sections

- Home — Introduction and call-to-action
- About — Background, academic experience, and social links
- Skills — Programming & Web, QA & Testing, Design & Analysis, Tools & Databases
- Projects — Featured academic and personal projects
- Certifications — Courses and training completed
- Contact — Ways to get in touch
